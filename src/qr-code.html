Login with QR Code

Login intoCardPay

Securely log in to your account by scanning the QR code using our mobile app. No password required.

How It Works

  • 1.Open the CardPay mobile app on your device.
  • 2.Tap the Profile icon from the top menu.
  • 3.Go to the Security section.
  • 4.Select QR Code Login.
  • 5.Use your phone to scan the QR code shown on this screen.
  • 6.Confirm the login request on your device to access your account

QR login is protected with device-level security and works only for logged-in, verified users.
