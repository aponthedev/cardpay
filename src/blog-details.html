A virtual card is a digital version of a payment card that is designed for online and digital transactions. Unlike physical cards, it exists only in digital form and comes with a unique card number, expiry date, and CVV.

Virtual cards are commonly used for:

Online shopping

Subscriptions and recurring payments

Digital services and platforms

How Does a Virtual Card Work?

Virtual cards work just like traditional cards but with added control and security.

Here’s how it works step by step:

You create a virtual card from your dashboard.

The card is linked to your wallet balance.

You use the card details for online payments.

Transactions are processed instantly.

This reduces the risk of fraud and unauthorized use.

How We Protect Your Data During Every Survey

Virtual cards are built with security in mind. You can:

Freeze or unfreeze the card anytime

Set spending limits

Terminate the card if suspicious activity is detected

Keep your main wallet details hidden

This reduces the risk of fraud and unauthorized use.
